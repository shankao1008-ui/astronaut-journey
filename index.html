關於《太空人的太陽系之旅 · 步行版》

第一人稱視角的太空人步行旅程。每一站降落在真實可站立的天體上:太陽與氣態巨行星沒有固態地表,所以太陽站改在隔熱觀景艙,木星、土星、天王星、海王星則降落在它們的衛星(木衛二、土衛二、天衛五、海衛一),巨大的行星掛在天上。

操作:降落後太空人會自動一直往前走;拖曳畫面環顧四周並改變前進方向;按 Enter 依該天體重力跳躍。鍵盤:S / ↓ 按住暫停前進,A D / ← → 轉向,Q E 抬頭低頭,Enter 或空白鍵跳,N / P 下一站 / 上一站。

誠實標示:場景為積木風格的示意圖;距離已縮短;天空中天體的大小依真實角直徑估算(小於 1.5° 的天體放大到 1.5° 以便辨識);系外行星地表外觀為依大小、溫度與主星推估。作品採用一般積木風格,與任何積木品牌無關。

About "An Astronaut's Solar System Walk"

A first-person walking tour. Every stop lands on a body you could really stand on: the Sun and the gas giants have no solid surface, so the Sun stop stays in a heat-shielded observation deck, and Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune are visited from their moons (Europa, Enceladus, Miranda, Triton) with the giant planet hanging in the sky.

Controls: after landing the astronaut keeps walking forward; drag the view to look around and steer; press Enter to jump with the local gravity. Keyboard: hold S / ↓ to pause, A D / ← → turn, Q E look up/down, Enter or Space jump, N / P next / previous stop.

Honest labelling: scenes are brick-style illustrations; distances are compressed; sky objects use their true angular diameter (objects under 1.5° are enlarged to 1.5° for visibility); exoplanet surfaces are inferred from size, temperature and host star. Generic brick style, unaffiliated with any brick brand.

Sources

  • NASA Solar System Exploration (planet and moon data)
  • NASA/JPL Horizons (orbital data)
  • NASA Exoplanet Archive (exoplanet parameters)
  • ESA Gaia (stellar distances)
  • Missions: Apollo, Venera, New Horizons, Cassini, Juno, Voyager 2, Hubble Space Telescope
